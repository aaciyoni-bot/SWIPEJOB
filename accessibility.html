Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 28 July 2026
RELJOB (operated by ORIZIS TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED) is committed to making our website usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines.

Measures we take

  • Semantic HTML with proper headings, landmarks and labelled form fields.
  • Keyboard navigation for interactive elements, with visible focus states.
  • Colour contrast chosen for readability, with meaning never conveyed by colour alone.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images; decorative images hidden from screen readers.
  • A responsive layout that works on phones, tablets and desktops.
  • Respect for “reduced motion” system settings.

Known limitations

Some content is provided by third parties (e.g. fonts, payment and sign-in widgets) and may not fully meet our standards. We work to improve these over time.

Give us feedback

If you find any part of RELJOB difficult to use, please tell us so we can fix it. Contact aaci.yoni@gmail.com or WhatsApp support, and describe the problem and the page. We aim to respond within a few business days.
