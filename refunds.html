Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026
This policy explains how RELJOB (operated by ORIZIS TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED) handles cancellations and refunds for memberships paid by Mobile Money.

1. Cancelling your membership

You can cancel at any time by contacting us at office@orizisgroup.com. Cancelling stops any future renewal. Your access remains until the end of the period you already paid for.

2. When you can get a refund

  • Duplicate or accidental charge (e.g. you were charged twice): full refund of the extra charge.
  • Charged but membership not activated due to a technical error on our side: full refund or activation, your choice.
  • Within 48 hours of your first payment, if we have not yet begun providing consulting to you: full refund on request.

3. When a refund does not apply

Because we sell a consulting service, once our team has started reviewing your profile or providing guidance for a paid period, that period is generally non-refundable. RELJOB never guarantees a job, visa or relocation, so an outcome not meeting your hopes is not, by itself, grounds for a refund.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned to the same Mobile Money number used for payment, normally within 5–10 business days, subject to the mobile network and our payment provider (pawaPay).

5. How to request a refund

Email office@orizisgroup.com with your name, the Mobile Money number used, the amount, and the date of payment. We aim to respond within 3 business days.

6. Contact

office@orizisgroup.com · email us (see site footer).
