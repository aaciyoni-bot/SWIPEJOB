Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026
This policy explains what personal data RELJOB (operated by ORIZIS TECHNOLOGY) collects, why, and your rights. We process data in line with the Zambia Data Protection Act, No. 3 of 2021.

1. Who is responsible

RELJOB / ORIZIS TECHNOLOGY, registration no. [company registration no.], of [registered address], is the data controller. Contact: aaci.yoni@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Profile details you submit: full name, age, gender, phone/WhatsApp, country and city, marital status, number of children, and (for couple applications) partner details.
  • A photo of yourself that you upload (not a CV).
  • Questionnaire answers: experience, languages, availability, passport status and motivation.
  • Account data if you sign in with Google (name, email, profile picture).
  • Payment reference: the Mobile Money number and transaction reference used for your membership.
  • Basic technical data (device/browser) if analytics are enabled.

3. How we use it

  • To provide relocation consulting and contact you about your membership and next steps.
  • To process and verify your Mobile Money membership payment.
  • To improve our service and prevent fraud or misuse.

4. Payment data

Mobile Money payments are processed by our provider (pawaPay) and the mobile networks. We receive a transaction reference and status — we never see or store your Mobile Money PIN or full financial credentials.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share your details only with our own relocation consultants/team, and with service providers who help us operate (e.g. payment and email delivery), under confidentiality. We may share your profile with a vetted opportunity partner only with your consent.

6. Storage & retention

We keep your data only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymise it. You can ask us to delete it sooner (see below).

7. Your rights

Under the Zambia Data Protection Act, 2021 you may request to access, correct, or delete your data, or object to certain uses. Email aaci.yoni@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Cookies & analytics

We may use basic cookies/analytics to understand usage and improve the site. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

9. Children

RELJOB is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes & contact

We may update this policy; the “last updated” date shows the latest version. Questions or requests: aaci.yoni@gmail.com or WhatsApp support.
